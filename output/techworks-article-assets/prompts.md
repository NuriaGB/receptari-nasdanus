# TechWorks TW26-01 Image Prompts

## Hero

Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: wide editorial hero image for a thought-leadership article
Primary request: Create a premium editorial hero illustration about the invisible organisational infrastructure behind innovation, without showing technology itself.
Scene/backdrop: dark navy editorial background (#07121F) with generous negative space for article typography.
Subject: a living organisational ecosystem: a small number of specialist communities gradually connect together from left to right, evolving from small autonomous clusters into one larger sophisticated connected organisation.
Style/medium: premium editorial illustration, refined abstract-organic geometry, closer to Financial Times, MIT Technology Review, and IBM editorial illustration than technology marketing artwork.
Composition/framing: wide 16:9 composition; organic network grows naturally left-to-right; keep the center-right visually rich but leave usable negative space on the upper-left/left third for typography.
Lighting/mood: calm, intelligent, elegant, human, mature.
Color palette: dark navy base, thin white and soft blue connection lines, white/pale blue/muted cyan nodes, subtle restrained accents of muted violet and teal for warmth.
Materials/textures: crisp vector-like editorial shapes with faint paper-grain texture; clean but not sterile.
Constraints: represent knowledge sharing, relationships, evolution, collaboration, structure and growth. It must feel like an organisation alive, not a computer network.
Avoid: circuits, semiconductors, servers, AI graphics, neural-network cliches, futuristic interfaces, robots, factory imagery, glowing cyberpunk effects, stock business people, logos, text, watermark.

## Diagram 1

Create a premium editorial infographic. A central circle labelled "Innovation". Around it, six supporting foundation pillars: People, Information, Processes, Connected Systems, Knowledge and Traceability. The visual metaphor should resemble structural foundations beneath a building rather than software architecture. Thin geometric lines, dark navy background, white and muted blue palette with restrained cyan/violet accents. Minimal, elegant, editorial. One idea only: innovation depends on an invisible operational foundation.

## Diagram 2

Create an editorial infographic showing three fundamentally different operational structures converging into one connected capability. People are represented as an organisational hierarchy, Processes as an engineering workflow, and Information as an interconnected knowledge network. All three connect into one central hexagon labelled "Operational Capability". After converging, subtle interconnections appear between the three domains, showing they no longer operate independently. Avoid glowing effects. Premium editorial style on dark background using white, blue, cyan and muted violet.

## Diagram 3

Create a clean editorial vertical progression with five stages: Idea, Engineering, Operational Capability, Repeatability and Sustainable Innovation. Each stage becomes visually more structured and connected. The transition should feel evolutionary rather than procedural. Minimal text, strong visual hierarchy, dark background, consistent with previous diagrams.
